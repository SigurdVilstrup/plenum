# v4 independent mark prompts

Shared prefix for every mark (append the geometry block):

```
Single app favicon logo mark only, square 1:1. Pure black and white, no gray, no color, no gradient. Solid black background. Centered white geometric icon only — no text, no letters, no wordmark, no app tile frame, no soft squircle container, no drop shadow.

Style: bold minimal SaaS / developer-tool mark like Cursor or Palantir icon sheets — thick mass, high contrast, readable at 16px. Solid fill, one weight, no outlines, no hairlines.
```

Reference sheets: `ref-sheet-c-bw.png`, `ref-sheet-e.png`

---

## 1. parallel-bands (abstract)

GEOMETRY — parallel-bands: exactly three identical solid white horizontal bars (capsules with slightly rounded ends), stacked vertically and perfectly centered. Each bar about 70% of canvas width and about 12% of canvas height. Vertical gaps between bars equal the bar thickness (wide, favicon-safe). Flat solid fill, one weight, no outlines, no hairlines, no inner slits.

## 2. split-rail (product — fork)

GEOMETRY — split-rail (fork metaphor): one thick vertical white rail on the left continuing straight top to bottom. From mid-height, a second same-weight white rail peels away upward-right at about 45 degrees. Flat-cut ends with real mass (no sharp hairline tip). Gap between stem and branch at least as wide as half the stroke. Single solid shapes, bold fill, no outlines.

Must NOT look like a letter Y, K, or a fast-forward chevron.

## 3. dual-bend (abstract)

GEOMETRY — dual-bend: exactly two parallel thick white tracks of equal stroke weight. Path: horizontal on the left, then a hard 45-degree diagonal bend, then horizontal again on the upper right (like a stepped ramp, not a soft S-curve). Gap between the two tracks equals the stroke thickness. Flat terminals on the left; slightly rounded on the right. Solid fill only.

Must NOT read as letter S or F or an escalator icon.

## 4. offset-bar (product — outboard)

GEOMETRY — offset-bar (outboard metaphor): two solid white vertical bars only. Left bar full height (~70% of canvas). Right bar same stroke width, shorter (~75% of left height), aligned to the bottom, offset to the right with a clear gap equal to about half a bar width. Slightly rounded rectangle ends. No third element. No hexagon outline, no brackets.

## 5. trunk-step (product — trunkside)

GEOMETRY — trunk-step: three solid white vertical bars stepping to the right. Left trunk bar tallest and thickest (full height). Middle bar shorter. Right bar shortest. All bottoms aligned on a shared baseline. Flat-cut tops (not round capsule tips). Equal gaps between bars roughly equal to bar width. No mirrored symmetry like an audio EQ meter — clearly weighted to the left trunk.

## 6. turnout (product — diverge)

GEOMETRY — turnout (rail switch): one thick vertical white stem continuing straight from bottom to top. From about mid-stem, one same-weight arm splits off diagonally up-right at 45 degrees. Arms are NOT mirrored — only one branch. Flat-cut ends with mass. No center joint detail, no pivot dot, no ring.

Must NOT look like letter Y, r, or pliers.

## 7. slit-row (abstract)

GEOMETRY — slit-row: three equal solid white rounded squares in a horizontal row. One continuous thick 45-degree black diagonal slit cuts through all three blocks (negative space), gap width at least as thick as a quarter of each square. Blocks remain clearly separate with white mass on both sides of the slit. No thin lines, no outline strokes.

## 8. feed-bars (abstract)

GEOMETRY — feed-bars: solid white half-disc (D-shape) on the left. Three bold solid white horizontal bars exit from the flat right edge of the disc, stacked with gaps equal to bar thickness, bars flush-merged into the disc (no hairline seam). Bars extend to the right, equal length, rounded ends. No nested outlines, no fork prongs, no cutlery look.

## 9. ref-stack (abstract)

GEOMETRY — ref-stack: three solid white horizontal bars, left-aligned. Top bar full width (the reference). Middle bar about 70% width. Bottom bar about 45% width. Bar height equal; vertical gap between bars equal to bar height. Slightly rounded rectangle ends. Solid fill only. No arcs, no ticks, no radar.

## 10. cross-two (abstract)

GEOMETRY — cross-two: exactly two thick white strands that cross once near the center. At the crossing, a clear black mask gap (weaving over/under) at least as wide as half the stroke — NOT a solid X blob. No six-lobe knot, no rotational flower symmetry, no OpenAI-style interlaced rosette. Asymmetric terminals OK. Flat solid strokes with mass.
